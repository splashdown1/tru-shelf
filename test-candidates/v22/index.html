What changed in this candidate

  • define now gives a short exact Strong’s glossary response; word study keeps the expanded lexicon, topic and dictionary detail.
  • Single-word definition and study queries use exact indexed matches, so ordinary “English” cannot drift into an unrelated Strong’s entry.
  • Corrected H157, G25 and G26 definitions/counts against Blue Letter Bible; removed unsupported claims that G26 means “unconditional love” or is lexically distinct from philia and eros.
  • Cleaned Nave’s Love display: restored “OF MAN FOR JESUS” from the scanned source, rendered See references and subentries cleanly.
  • Corrected Easton’s opening quotation mark and the damaged Thayer G26 introduction against their source text.
  • Voice status now reports the number of local English voices and the actual AUTO/selected voice; it no longer claims none exist just because gender names are unrecognised.
  • Retains the mathematics boundary, explicit H4962 lookup, earlier lexicon repairs and voice-arrival refresh.

Useful checks

  1. Try define love for the concise result and word study love for the full study.
  2. Try define English and word study English; both should report no exact local entry rather than guessing.
  3. Try define Strong's H157 and word study Strong's H157 to check concise and full explicit-ID routes.
  4. Try define math and tell me about math; then ask what should we discuss? and verify math is not suggested.
  5. Try topic: love and confirm the “OF MAN FOR JESUS” heading and readable See/list formatting.
  6. On your device, check the voice count, AUTO voice name and voice test. The test browser exposed no local English voices, so audible playback remains device-specific.
