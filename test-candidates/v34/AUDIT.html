TRU v34 audit

Human-test candidate for the light/dark display toggle, contrast, control separation and control descriptions.

Change set

  • Dark mode remains the default; a LIGHT/DARK toggle changes theme and persists the choice locally.
  • Light mode uses darker text across ready state, cards, metadata and controls. The audited text-pair contrast minimum is 6.07:1.
  • Chair and TRU Portal are separated on desktop and moved into a dedicated header row on narrow screens.
  • Chair and Portal have descriptive accessible names; the existing controls retain their behavior.
  • Portal identifies the app as v34.

Verification

  • Deterministic v34 build from the pinned v33 artifact; exact source hash recorded in README.md.
  • Byte-level verifier confirms no other app bytes changed outside the build stamp and v34 display/control patch.
  • Contrast verifier: minimum 6.07:1 for checked text/background pairs.
  • Local browser checks: saved theme survives reload; Chair and Portal open; desktop and phone-width controls do not overlap; no browser errors.
  • Public Pages verification is recorded after deployment.

Limits

Local smoke tests are not user acceptance. This candidate does not alter TRU knowledge or its underlying data. It remains an artifact-based candidate and does not resolve the missing matching v17 modular source tree.
