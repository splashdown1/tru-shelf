Why this change

The user's earlier thoughts about forgiveness query returned a GAP. The requested fix is a focused card of Jesus' recorded words on forgiveness, not an AI-generated doctrinal answer.

v33 adds a direct response for forgiveness prompts and appends the same card to word study forgiveness. Existing lexicon, topical, dictionary and deep-entry results remain in place.

Quotation set

Ten selected KJV quotations or excerpts from six passages marked in the bundled red-letter index:

  • Matthew 6:14–15
  • Matthew 18:22
  • Mark 11:25
  • Luke 17:3–4
  • Luke 23:34
  • Luke 7:47–48, 50

Each displayed line is read from the bundled KJV by reference. Luke 23:34 is a direct speech-only excerpt (“Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do.”), excluding the narrator's clause. The card adds no paraphrase. The displayed citations are clickable and open the corresponding local verse.
